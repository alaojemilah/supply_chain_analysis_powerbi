## Analysis Overview
This project presents an E-Commerce Sales Analysis built in Excel as part of the TechCrush Data Analytics training, scoring 10/10. The dataset (361 rows) covers global e-commerce sales across multiple countries, product categories, and time periods. The analysis combines data from separate Sales, Product, and Customer Info tables using lookup functions, and presents the results through an interactive dashboard with slicers for country and category.

---

## Objective
- Combine data from multiple related tables into a single working dataset
- Clean and standardize raw sales data for accurate analysis
- Identify sales trends by category, country, day of the week, and month
- Build an interactive dashboard allowing users to filter by country and category

---

## Data Source
- Source Type: Practice dataset provided by TechCrush tutor for an Excel project
- Total Records: 361 rows
- Tables: Sales, Product, Customer_Info
- Dimensions Covered: Category, Country, Day of Week, Month
- Metrics Tracked: Total Amount, Quantity Sold

---

## Preparation Tools
- Microsoft Excel: Data cleaning, lookups, formulas, dashboard creation
- Excel Functions: VLOOKUP, XLOOKUP, IF, TEXT
- Excel Tables & Slicers: Interactive filtering by Country and Category

---

## Data Processing

### Data Cleaning & Transformation
- Renamed columns for clarity and consistency
- Standardized currency formatting across the amount column
- Fixed inconsistent date formats
- Removed duplicate records

### Data Modeling
- Set up relationships between the Sales table and the Product and Customer_Info tables
- Used VLOOKUP and XLOOKUP to pull product and customer-related information into the Sales table
- Used TEXT and IF functions to derive Month and Day of Week columns from the Date field

---

## Skills Demonstrated
- Data Cleaning: Deduplication, currency formatting standardization, date correction
- Lookup Functions: VLOOKUP, XLOOKUP for cross-table data integration
- Formulas: IF statements, TEXT functions for derived columns
- Data Visualization: Dashboard charts with interactive slicers
- Data Modeling: Combining multiple tables into a unified analysis view

---

## Insights
- Electronics is the top-performing category by both revenue and quantity sold, far ahead of other categories
- The United Kingdom leads total revenue by country, followed by Canada and the United States
- The UK and Canada together account for 57% of total quantity sold (35% and 22% respectively)
- Sales peak in March and again in June, with a dip from April to May
- Tuesday is the strongest sales day, notably outperforming all other days of the week

---

## Recommendations
- Prioritize inventory and marketing investment in Electronics given its dominant performance
- Investigate why the UK and Canada outperform other markets and apply learnings to underperforming regions like India and the US
- Plan promotions and stock levels around the March and June sales peaks, and address the April-May dip
- Explore what drives Tuesday's strong performance and consider replicating those conditions on other days

## Analysis Overview
This project presents a Supply Chain Analysis built in Power BI as part of Power BI training. The dataset (100 rows) tracks $577.6K in total revenue across products, locations, and shipping carriers. The dashboard surfaces top-performing products and locations, monitors stock levels and revenue by SKU, compares shipping carrier performance, and flags defect rates by product type.

---

## Objective
- Identify top-performing products and locations by revenue
- Monitor stock levels across SKUs to flag potential inventory issues
- Compare shipping carrier performance by revenue generated
- Track average defect rates across product types to highlight quality concerns
- Provide a filterable dashboard for stakeholders to explore performance by location and transportation method

---

## Data Source
- Source Type: Practice dataset provided by tutor for Power BI training
- Total Records: 100 rows
- Dimensions Covered: Product Type, Location, SKU, Shipping Carrier
- Metrics Tracked: Revenue, Stock Levels, Defect Rates

- Power BI Desktop: Dashboard development and visualizations
- Power Query: Data cleaning and formatting

### Data Modeling
- Used a single flat table structure, with no relationships or DAX measures needed for this analysis

## Skills Demonstrated
- Data Cleaning: Currency formatting
- Data Visualization: Bar charts, line charts, KPI cards

## Insights
- Total revenue across the dataset is $577.6K
- Skincare is the top-performing product type by revenue
- Mumbai is the top-performing location by revenue
- Carrier B generates the highest revenue ($0.25M) among shipping carriers, followed by Carrier C and Carrier A
- Stock levels remain relatively stable across most SKUs, ranging between 93 and 100 units
- Haircare has the highest average defect rate (2.5), followed by skincare (2.3) and cosmetics (1.9)

---

## Recommendations
- Prioritize skincare and Mumbai-based operations given their strong revenue performance
- Investigate why Carrier B outperforms other carriers and consider expanding reliance on it where appropriate
- Address quality issues in haircare production given its higher defect rate compared to other product types
- Monitor SKUs with declining stock levels (e.g. SKU45, SKU7) to prevent potential stockouts
